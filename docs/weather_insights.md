

# AtmoSync Weather Insights

## Purpose

This document records important observations from the AtmoSync micro-climate dataset analysis.

## 1. Temperature Insights

* Calculate average maximum temperature.

* Calculate average minimum temperature.

* Compare temperature across locations.

* Identify temperature variations over time.

## 2. Rainfall Insights

* Calculate average rainfall.

* Calculate total rainfall.

* Compare rainfall across locations.

* Identify date-wise rainfall patterns.

## 3. Humidity Insights

* Calculate average humidity.

* Identify minimum and maximum humidity.

* Compare humidity across locations.

## 4. Wind Speed Insights

* Calculate average wind speed.

* Identify minimum and maximum wind speed.

* Compare wind speed across locations.

## 5. Location-wise Comparison

Compare temperature, rainfall, humidity, and wind speed across the locations available in the dataset.

## 6. Weather Relationships

Use correlation analysis to study relationships between temperature, rainfall, humidity, and wind speed.

Correlation does not necessarily indicate causation.

## 7. Recording Findings

For every finding, record:

* Weather parameter

* Relevant location or date

* Actual result from the analysis

* Supporting statistic or chart

## Final Objective

These insights will support the AtmoSync dashboard, project report, and final presentation.
